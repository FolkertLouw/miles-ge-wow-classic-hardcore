# Tauren Hunter 1–10 Route Notes

## Status

Starter route page. Expand only from personally verified field data or clearly marked external reference data.

## Current DaddyStitch Goal

Reach level 5, get to Bloodhoof Village, train Skinning and Leatherworking, and buy a Skinning Knife.

## Known Early Principles

- Before level 10, Hunter has no pet; play conservatively.
- Avoid caves and multi-pulls unless there is a strong reason.
- Gold is scarce; training, repairs, ammo, and profession startup costs matter.
- Beasts killed before Skinning are useful for XP, but do not provide leather until Skinning is trained.
